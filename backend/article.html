How do I update my personal information?

You can update the personal details of your Flexischools account via the Flexischools Application or Website.

Also applicable to CDFpay App

This can be done by selecting the "Profile" tab, then selecting "Manage my details" .

In this section you will be able to change the information of your Flexischools account including your Name, Title, Email Address and Mobile Number.

Here you can also update your preferred payment method or close your Flexischools account.

Please be aware that if you change the email to your Flexischools account, this is the email that you will be required to log in with.
